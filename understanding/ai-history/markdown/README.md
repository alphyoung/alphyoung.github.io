# 从符号主义到智能体的技术演进综述

_Seventy Years of Artificial Intelligence: A Technical Review from Symbolism to Agentic Systems_

`1936 — 2026` `七章 · 两个专题` `77 条参考文献` `资料截至 2026 年 9 月`

***

## 摘要

本文对人工智能自 1936 年计算理论奠基至 2026 年智能体规模化部署的发展历程进行系统综述。全文以"预期与现实的落差"为主线，将约九十年的演进划分为奠基、符号主义黄金期、专家系统、连接主义复兴、深度学习革命、大模型与智能体七个阶段，并另设人机交互入口迁移与检索增强生成（RAG）两个专题，分别考察"人如何用上 AI"与"生成内容从何而来"两条与技术史并行的线索。综述识别出五条贯穿性规律：技术寒冬的成因是预期崩塌而非能力退步；范式竞争的胜负由可扩展性而非当下性能决定；数据、算力与算法三要素须同时到位方能引发革命；基础设施的溢出效应常被系统性低估；治理节奏系统性地滞后于能力扩散。文章另对截至 2026 年 9 月仍在推进的主要研究方向做分层汇总，按认知、系统、基础、治理四层逐项标注成熟度与主要约束，并指出制约条件最多的两个方向——世界模型与可解释性——恰是决定长期上限的方向。文章认为，2022 年以后的大模型与智能体转向是人工智能史上第一次未出现显著预期落差的技术周期，但任务规划可靠性、业务系统对接复杂度与权责界定三项约束尚未解决，构成本轮周期的主要不确定性。

**关键词**　人工智能史 · 符号主义 · 连接主义 · 深度学习 · 大语言模型 · 检索增强生成 · 智能体

## Abstract

This review surveys the development of artificial intelligence from the theoretical foundations laid in 1936 to the large-scale deployment of agentic systems in 2026. Organised around the recurring gap between expectation and capability, it divides roughly ninety years into seven technological stages — foundations, the symbolic golden age, expert systems, the connectionist revival, the deep learning revolution, the era of large models, and the era of generative AI and agents — and adds two thematic reviews on the migration of human–AI interaction entry points and on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Five regularities are identified: winters are caused by collapsed expectations rather than regressed capability; paradigm competition is settled by scalability rather than present-day performance; data, compute and algorithms must converge simultaneously; the spillover effects of infrastructure are systematically underestimated; and governance systematically lags capability diffusion. The post-2022 shift toward large models and agents is argued to be the first cycle in the field's history without a pronounced expectation gap, though task-planning reliability, enterprise system integration and accountability remain unresolved.

**Keywords**　history of artificial intelligence · symbolism · connectionism · deep learning · large language models · retrieval-augmented generation · AI agents
