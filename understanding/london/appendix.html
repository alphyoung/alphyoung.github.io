Three questions to ask of any ruin

Is what you see the original? Which period does it belong to? Is the conclusion based on the building, on archaeology, or on later legend? London often layers Roman, medieval and modern remains on the same spot, and buildings standing next to each other are not therefore the same age.

III. Table of English and British dynasties

A dynasty is the family a monarch belongs to; it is not the same as the name of a state. Before 1707 this is mainly the history of English dynasties; after 1707 it is Great Britain, and from 1801 the United Kingdom period.
